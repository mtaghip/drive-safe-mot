Services and prices

Straightforward work, fair prices.

01

MOT test

A full MOT carried out to the DVSA standard. We check brakes, lights, steering, suspension, tyres, seatbelts, emissions and more, then talk you through the result and any advisories.

  • ✓Takes around an hour, wait or drop off
  • ✓Cars, small vans and motorhomes
  • ✓Result and advisories explained in plain English
£45
Fixed price
02

DPF clean

If your diesel is showing a DPF warning light, going into limp mode or losing power, a blocked diesel particulate filter is a common cause. We clean the filter to restore flow, which is often far cheaper than replacing it.

  • ✓For diesel cars and vans
  • ✓Warning light and fault code check
  • ✓Helps with emissions on your MOT
From
£150
03

MOT works

Failed your MOT, or have advisories you'd like sorted? We carry out the repairs needed to get your vehicle through, and give you a price before any work begins.

  • ✓Brakes, tyres, lights and wipers
  • ✓Suspension, steering and exhausts
  • ✓Retest after repairs
Quoted
Before any work starts

Tyre change
